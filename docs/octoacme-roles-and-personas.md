# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers create intuitive and accessible user interfaces by combining user research, design principles, and close collaboration with product and engineering teams. They ensure that solutions meet user needs and deliver a high-quality experience.

### Responsibilities
- Design user interfaces and interaction flows
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Prototype solutions and iterate based on feedback
- Collaborate with Product Managers on requirements alignment
- Work with Developers on design handoff and implementation feedback
- Partner with QA on usability acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Deliver user-centered designs that improve satisfaction and adoption
- Reduce friction and support accessibility across all user touchpoints
- Maintain a consistent design language across the product

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and prototype walkthroughs with Product and Engineering
- Usability test reports and research summaries
- Design specs and annotated mockups shared via design tooling

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads own the quality assurance strategy for a project, defining and executing test plans to ensure all quality gates are met before release. They act as an advocate for quality across the entire delivery lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain overall test strategy and test plans
- Coordinate test execution across unit, integration, and acceptance levels
- Ensure quality gates are met prior to each release milestone
- Provide testability feedback to Developers during design and implementation
- Collaborate with Project Managers on release readiness criteria
- Partner with Customer Success on bug triage priority

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Improve test coverage and reduce regression risk
- Enable confident, predictable releases

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews and quality gate sign-offs
- Bug reports, triage sessions, and defect tracking
- Release readiness reports shared with Project Managers

---

## Solution Architects

### Role Summary
Solution Architects define and govern the technical architecture of a project, ensuring that implementation decisions align with business goals, scalability requirements, and security standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define architectural patterns and technology standards
- Ensure technical decisions align with business goals and long-term system health
- Review implementation plans for scalability, reliability, and security
- Provide technical oversight and guidance to Developers
- Communicate systems impact and architectural trade-offs to Stakeholders
- Collaborate with Product and Project Managers on requirements and constraints

### Goals
- Deliver a scalable, maintainable, and secure technical foundation
- Reduce technical debt through proactive architectural governance
- Align technical direction with business strategy

### Typical Communication
- Architecture decision records (ADRs) and technical design reviews
- Implementation plan reviews with engineering teams
- Systems impact briefings for Product/Project Managers and Stakeholders

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business stakeholders and technical teams by gathering, documenting, and translating requirements into actionable user stories and specifications that guide development.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements through stakeholder interviews and workshops
- Translate business needs into user stories, acceptance criteria, or functional specifications
- Analyze and recommend process improvements
- Collaborate with Product Managers on backlog refinement and requirements
- Validate requirements with Stakeholders before handoff to development
- Provide clarifications to Developers and support QA with acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Ensure requirements are clear, complete, and aligned with business objectives
- Reduce rework caused by ambiguous or incomplete specifications
- Support continuous process improvement across the delivery lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation, user stories, and process flow diagrams
- Stakeholder validation workshops and sign-off sessions
- Backlog refinement sessions with Product Managers and Developers

---

## Customer Success Managers

### Role Summary
Customer Success Managers represent the voice of the end user within the project team. They monitor adoption and satisfaction metrics, advise on release readiness from a customer perspective, and ensure post-release health is maintained.

### Responsibilities
- Represent end-user feedback and customer needs in project discussions
- Advise on release readiness based on customer impact and risk
- Monitor customer satisfaction and adoption metrics post-release
- Partner with QA on edge cases and real-world usage scenarios
- Collaborate with Product Managers on feature requests and roadmap priorities
- Provide Stakeholders with post-release health updates

### Goals
- Maximize customer satisfaction and product adoption
- Ensure releases are ready from the customer's perspective
- Surface actionable feedback to continuously improve the product

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and adoption dashboards
- Release readiness input to Project and Product Managers
- Post-release health reports for Stakeholders

---

## Stakeholder Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Sponsors provide executive-level support for the project, ensuring it has the necessary resources, funding, and organizational backing. They remove strategic roadblocks and maintain alignment between the project and broader business priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Provide executive sponsorship and organizational support for the project
- Ensure adequate resourcing and funding throughout delivery
- Remove strategic roadblocks and escalation items that cannot be resolved at the team level
- Maintain alignment between project objectives and broader business strategy
- Engage with Project Managers on status updates and escalations
- Collaborate with Product Managers on strategic direction and trade-offs

### Goals
- Ensure the project has the organizational support needed to succeed
- Align project outcomes with executive and business priorities
- Enable rapid resolution of high-priority blockers

### Typical Communication
- Executive status briefings from Project Managers
- Strategic alignment reviews with Product Managers
- Escalation and decision-making forums with the broader stakeholder group
